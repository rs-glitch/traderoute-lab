    What this tool will and won't tell you

    • Forecasts are ranges with probabilities, checked against history for calibration. They are never a single target.
    • Every trading signal is graded out of sample against an exposure-matched buy & hold and a t > 3 multiple-testing bar. Most fail, which is what the research predicts.
    • Trade routes size positions to your risk budget, with stops, and show P(target first) next to P(stop first).
    • Paper trading only. No orders ever leave your browser.
